About ThunderStudy AI

Free education, built by Wondermayank

ThunderStudy AI is a free educational project run by Wondermayank, a solo student developer building free educational tools since 2018. The site focuses on accessible AI experiences that help students study, practise and revise effectively.

Last updated: October 2, 2026 · By Wondermayank

Explore ThunderStudy AI Contact Wondermayank
Mission

Make useful learning tools free

The project focuses on accessible AI-powered learning experiences for students, including study assistance, mindmaps, flashcards, mock-test creation and formula-sheet generation.

What the site offers

ThunderStudy AI brings together AI tools for study and exam preparation. The wider AI Lab also links to Tiny AI Chat and Self Care AI for everyday use.
