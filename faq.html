ThunderStudy AI FAQ

Questions, answered

ThunderStudy AI provides free educational AI tools for study and exam preparation. This FAQ explains who runs the project, how the tools are intended to be used, account expectations, support, availability and whether the site shows ads.

Last updated: October 2, 2026 · By Wondermayank

Yes. The tools presented on ThunderStudy AI are intended as free educational tools. Individual third-party services may have their own availability or usage limits.

ThunderStudy AI is run by Wondermayank, a solo student developer building free educational tools since 2018.

The AI Lab includes AI Chatbot Study, AI Mindmap, AI Flashcard, AI Mock Test Maker and AI Formula Sheet.

The site describes its study tools around Indian competitive-exam preparation, including CUET, SSC, Banking and UPSC.

It is an exam-oriented AI study chatbot designed to answer questions and provide exam-ready explanations.

It turns a topic or chapter into a visual mind map to support faster review and organization.

It generates revision flashcards from notes or topics so students can practice recall.

It generates full-length mock tests for competitive-exam practice.

It generates concise, exam-ready formula sheets for a subject or chapter.

The AI Lab page says most tools work without an account; some ThunderStudy tools may request a Google sign-in to sync progress.

Email contact@wondermayank.indevs.in or use the Contact link in the footer.

No. The project explicitly states in ads.txt that it does not show or sell ads.
